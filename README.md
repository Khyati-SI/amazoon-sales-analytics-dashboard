
#  Amazon Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Excel-based Sales Analytics Dashboard** built to analyze Amazon sales data across products, regions, customers, payment methods, and delivery performance.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using **Excel, PivotTables, XLOOKUP, formulas, PivotCharts, slicers, and dashboard KPIs**.

---

##  Project Overview

This project was created as part of a data analytics mini-project focused on transforming raw Amazon sales transactions into an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

The dataset contains **100 sales transactions** covering multiple:

- Products
- Categories
- Regions
- Customers
- Payment methods
- Order dates
- Delivery dates
- Delivery statuses
- Sales amounts

The objective was to clean and analyze the data, calculate relevant business metrics, identify sales and fulfillment patterns, and present the findings through an interactive Excel dashboard.
---
## 📋 Project Guidelines & Requirements

This project was completed as part of the **Mini Project – Week 03: Workforce & Sales Intelligence Dashboard**.

The project requirements included:

- Cleaning and standardizing the Amazon sales dataset
- Structuring date, region, category, payment, and delivery fields
- Using **XLOOKUP** to retrieve and map customer/product/region information
- Creating calculated fields using Excel formulas
- Building **PivotTables** for sales, regions, categories, delivery status, and cancellations
- Analyzing delivery performance, including orders delivered within 2 days
- Calculating cancellation rate by region
- Calculating effective sales based on delivered orders
- Creating **PivotCharts and visualizations**
- Adding interactive **filters/slicers** for Region, Payment Method, Delivery Status, and Month
- Designing an interactive dashboard with KPI cards
- Identifying business insights and providing actionable recommendations

### Required Deliverables

The project deliverables included:

##  Business Objective

The dashboard is designed to answer key business questions such as:

- Which product category generates the highest revenue?
- Which region has the highest cancellation rate?
- How many orders are delivered within 2 days?
- What are the most successful regions?
- What is the effective sales value from delivered orders?
- What is the overall cancellation rate?
- How is the order volume distributed across delivery statuses?
- Which areas of fulfillment performance require further investigation?
